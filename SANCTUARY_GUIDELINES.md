# Gentlewick Sanctuary Guidelines & Moderation Policy

**Last updated:** 22 November 2026

Gentlewick is a quiet, compassionate space to remember cherished animal companions. By creating a memorial, lighting a candle, or leaving a message on gentlewick.com, you agree to follow these guidelines.

## 1. What is not allowed

To keep Gentlewick safe and respectful for grieving families, you must not post:
- Illegal content under Irish or EU law.
- Animal cruelty, abuse, neglect, or graphic violence.
- Harassment, hate speech, threats, or mockery directed at pet owners, visitors, or any group.
- Private personal information (doxxing), such as home addresses, phone numbers, or private contact details, without permission.
- Sexually explicit content, spam, commercial advertising, scams, or malware.
- Content that infringes copyright or privacy rights, including photographs you do not have permission to upload or share.

## 2. Moderation

Gentlewick may use automated anti-spam or content-safety tools when configured and available. Automated tools may make mistakes and are not a substitute for human review.

Reports and flagged content may be reviewed by a moderator. We may investigate reports, take action, and preserve or disclose information where required by law.

**Current implementation status:** Shared memorial creation remains disabled by default. A content-report form is now available from the shared memorial dialog and at `/report`; reports are stored privately in the database. Reports are not yet supported by email notifications, an automated screening pipeline, or an in-site moderation queue. Manual review and enforcement procedures must be established before shared memorial creation is enabled.

## 3. Enforcement and reasons

Where content violates these guidelines or applicable law, Gentlewick may remove or hide a memorial, photograph, or message; restrict posting; or block repeat offenders. Where reasonably possible and contact details are available, we will explain the action, the reason, and whether automated tools contributed to the decision. Legal requirements and urgent safety issues may affect when notice can be given.

## 4. Reporting content and contact

Until an in-site reporting tool is available, report concerns by emailing **hello@gentlewick.com**. Include the memorial or message link and a description of the concern. Please do not include unnecessary personal information.

Reports are accepted in English. We will review reports as reasonably practicable; this is not an emergency service.

## 5. Updates

We may update these guidelines as the service and moderation tools develop. The latest version will be published on gentlewick.com.
