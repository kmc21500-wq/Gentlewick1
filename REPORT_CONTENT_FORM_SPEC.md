# Report Content on Gentlewick — Form Specification

Use this form to notify us of content on gentlewick.com that you believe is illegal under Irish or EU law (or violates our Sanctuary Guidelines).

## Fields

1. **Link (URL) to the content being reported** — Required; auto-filled if opened from a memorial.
2. **Which specific item is affected?** — Required: Memorial text / Uploaded photo / Condolence message / Other.
3. **Reason for your report** — Required: Animal cruelty or graphic violence / Harassment, hate speech, or threats / Private personal data or doxxing / Copyright or privacy infringement / Spam or scam / Suspected child sexual abuse material (CSAM) / Other illegal content.
4. **Explain why you believe this content is illegal** — Required textarea; request a clear, specific explanation.
5. **Your Full Name** — Required, except optional when reporting suspected child sexual abuse offences.
6. **Your Email Address** — Required, except optional when reporting suspected child sexual abuse offences. Used for receipt confirmation and decision notification.
7. **Good-Faith Declaration** — Required checkbox: “I confirm that I have a good-faith belief that the information and allegations in this report are accurate and complete.”

Submit button: **Submit Report**

## What happens next

If an email address is provided, send an automated confirmation of receipt and notify the reporter of the decision once reviewed, including whether automated tools were used in handling the report.

## Implementation and safety notes

This is a specification, not a claim that the form or reporting backend is live. Do not publish it as a working reporting flow until secure report storage, notifications, moderation workflow, access controls, abuse prevention, retention, and escalation procedures are implemented and tested.

For suspected child sexual abuse material (CSAM), do not encourage reporters to download, copy, attach, or redistribute the material. The report form should accept the content URL and a description only, and route such reports for urgent, restricted-access handling under an appropriate legal procedure.
